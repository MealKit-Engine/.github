# Code of Conduct

MealKit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in all its repositories, issues, discussions and other community spaces.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, criticize ideas rather than people, and don't harass anyone. Sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at kingprotea99@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban people from the project, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
